# Niamind

> Life moves better together.

Niamind is a platform where people help each other move forward in life through reflection, connection, and contribution.

It is not a mental health platform. It is not a social network. It is not a productivity tool.

It exists to help people navigate life intentionally — alongside others who are doing the same.

---

## Why Niamind Exists

Most platforms are built to hold your attention.

Niamind is built to do the opposite. To give you something real, then send you back to your life.

The people Niamind is built for are doing fine by most measures. Things are moving. But somewhere along the way, the conversations that used to hold the real version of them got thinner. The inner life — the part that reflects, questions, and is quietly working on something — doesn't have many places built for it.

Niamind is being built to be one of those places.

---

## The Human Architecture

Everything Niamind builds toward follows one pattern:

> Reflect → Connect → Contribute → Move Forward

This is not a product funnel. It is a description of how human growth actually works.

People reflect on where they are. They connect with others navigating similar territory. They contribute what they have learned. And in doing so, they move forward — and help others do the same.

Every feature on Niamind serves at least one of these four movements.

---

## What Niamind Is

**Journey** — A private space for personal reflection. Notes, prompts, intentions, and curated content matched to what someone is working on. Complete on its own, before community exists.

**Feed** — Populated by Spaces the member has actively joined and Niamind editorial. Not infinite scroll. Has a natural endpoint.

**Spaces** — Communities organised around areas of life people are actively working on. Joined deliberately. Never pushed into a member's experience uninvited.

**Discussions** — Where lived experience becomes useful to others. Questions, reflections, resources, and honest stories.

**Connections** — Mutual, consent-based relationships. Not followers. Not an audience mechanic.

**Guidance** — Verified professionals who earn visibility through community contribution, not directory fees. Accessed when needed, not pushed.

---

## Core Principles

These are non-negotiable. They apply to every feature, every engineering decision, and every contribution.

- No follower counts
- No public popularity rankings
- No engagement leaderboards
- No gamification of personal growth
- No algorithmic attention competition
- Success is measured by usefulness to the member, not time spent on the platform

> Complexity belongs to the system, not the member.

Members should never need to understand how the platform works underneath. They should simply experience a community where showing up with genuine intent creates real forward movement.

## Contributing

Niamind is being built in public.

Contributions are welcome from people who understand what Niamind is trying to be — not just what it is trying to build. Before contributing, read this README in full. Then read the platform principles again. If a contribution would compromise them, it will not be merged regardless of technical quality.

**How to contribute:**

1. Open an issue describing your proposal and why it fits
2. Wait for discussion and alignment before writing any code
3. Submit a pull request referencing the issue

For anything beyond a small fix, start a conversation first. We would rather spend time aligning than reviewing work that goes in the wrong direction.

**Where help is needed right now:**

- Frontend development — React, Tailwind
- Product design and UX
- Copywriting and editorial
- Community building and onboarding thinking

We are not ready for contributions to infrastructure, backend systems, or the main application yet. Those repositories do not exist publicly. Opening pull requests for systems not in this repository will be closed.

---

## What We Are Not Building

Some things are explicitly not part of Niamind, now or in the future:

- A follower or audience system
- An engagement-optimised feed
- A gamified growth or habit tracker
- A crisis intervention or clinical mental health service
- A corporate productivity tool

If a proposed contribution moves toward any of these, it will not be accepted.
